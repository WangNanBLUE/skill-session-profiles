# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or
socioeconomic status.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact directly.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's data without permission.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations
may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent possible.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
